# How to publish an HTML5 game on klyo games

Sign in at dev.klyo.pl, upload a ZIP with index.html inside, play the game in the preview, describe it and press “Release to the catalogue”. The game works at its own address at once and enters the catalogue after a person from klyo has looked at it.

- 3 min read
- Steps: 6

### On this page

## Before you start

- A game that runs in a browser: a ZIP with index.html in its root, or a single HTML file, up to 350 MB.
- A free klyo account. The account you play with on games.klyo.pl opens the studio too.
- About ten minutes, most of it spent playing your own game.

## Step by step

1. **Open the studio and sign in**: Go to dev.klyo.pl. Under “I already have a klyo account” press **Sign in**, or fill in the form next to it and press **Create account**. The first time you add something, the studio shows the rules: tick **I have read and accept the rules** and press **Accept**.
2. **Upload the package**: In the **New game** form choose your file in the field for the ZIP with the game. The studio unpacks it and within seconds checks the size, the files, the paths, malicious code, third-party ad networks and connections outside klyo.
3. **Play it before anyone else**: The game opens in the preview. Switch between **Desktop** and **Phone**, try **Full screen**, or scan the code to play on your own phone; that address works only for you and expires after a day. Play for 15 to 30 seconds: the studio records in the background, and that round becomes the tile clip and the cover.
4. **Describe the game**: Press **Works, describe and submit**. Give the game a name of up to 30 characters (it becomes the address), a one-sentence tagline, a description in English of at least 25 words and, if you like, one in Polish for extra XP. Pick up to 3 genres and answer the 10 questions about the content. Have Claude or ChatGPT? **Fill with your AI** types the text while you watch.
5. **Submit and release**: Press **Upload and publish the game**. When the game card says the game is ready, press **Release to the catalogue**. The game works at its own address at once, and its page is usually built within a minute and a half. No clip yet? The card first asks you to **Play and record a clip**, because a game enters the catalogue with a clip.
6. **After the release**: A person from klyo plays every new developer's game, usually the same day, and only then does it enter the catalogue and search engines. Until then it opens only for adult accounts and the ads next to it stay off. Plays and visits appear on the game card live.

## If something goes wrong

### The package is refused

The check always names the reason: index.html missing from the root of the ZIP, a package over 350 MB, a third-party ad network in the code or a file loaded from someone else's server. Fix it and upload again.

### The preview shows a white screen

Press **Show what happened** in the preview. It lists the files the game asked for and did not get, and the errors from the browser. The white screen guide walks through every cause.

### There is no release button

The game needs a clip first. Press **Play and record a clip**, play for 15 to 30 seconds and the release button appears.

## How much does it cost to publish a game on klyo games?

Nothing. Publishing, hosting, the catalogue page, clips, leaderboards and statistics are free, and there is no exclusivity: the game can stay on other sites too.

## How long does it take to publish a game?

Usually about ten minutes from the ZIP to the release, most of it playing your own game. Then a person from klyo looks at the game, usually the same day, and it enters the catalogue.

## Can I publish a game exported from Godot or Unity?

Yes. Export it for the Web, zip the contents of the export folder (index.html in the root, next to the .wasm and .pck or the Build folder) and upload it. The ZIP can be 350 MB, 700 MB unpacked and 500 MB for a single file. Players download the engine and the game data from our CDN, with no change to your code.

## My game works only with a mouse and keyboard. Can I publish it?

Yes. In the game format pick **Computer only**. The game then does not show up on phones, and anyone who opens its page on a phone is offered to play it on a computer or send themselves the link.
