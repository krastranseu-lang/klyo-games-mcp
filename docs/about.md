# What *klyo games* is

klyo games is a free portal of browser games with TikTok-style clips, a community and records, and a studio where an independent developer publishes a game the same day. This page says plainly who runs it, how we make money and what stage we are at.

## What is inside

One account, one portal and eight parts that work together. Each one has its own page.

- **[Clips](https://games.klyo.pl/clips/)**: A vertical feed of short gameplay videos, with captions from your voice and reactions from your camera.
- **[Community](https://games.klyo.pl/community/)**: Posts with a game attached, a conversation page for every game and moderation with a public report.
- **[Records and ranks](https://games.klyo.pl/records/)**: Boards for the day and all time, medals and 17 ranks, with scores that come only from real rounds.
- **[Play together](https://games.klyo.pl/game-sdk/)**: Rooms with a code, quick matches and a ready-made lobby for turn-based and live games.
- **[Developer Studio](https://games.klyo.pl/developer-studio/)**: Uploads, a preview on four screens, versions, statistics and a creator page at dev.klyo.pl.
- **[AI assistants](https://games.klyo.pl/mcp/)**: An MCP server with 20 tools: publish and fix a game by talking to Claude or ChatGPT.
- **[klyo SDK](https://games.klyo.pl/game-sdk/)**: One line in a game adds scores, cloud saves, rewarded ads, clips and online play.
- **[Safety](https://games.klyo.pl/moderation-report/)**: A person reviews every game, age labels use the PEGI and Apple scales, and reports are decided within 24 hours.

## Three good ideas in one place

We did not invent a portal from scratch. We took three things that work separately elsewhere and put them under one account.

- **A vertical clip feed, like TikTok**: You thumb through short gameplay recordings, and the game only starts when you decide to start it. You record a clip in the browser with one click, with no software at all.
- **You publish yourself and stay the owner, like itch.io**: Upload a package or paste the address of a game that already exists. The rights stay yours, there is no exclusivity, and the game gets its own address.
- **The game starts on click and earns, like CrazyGames**: No store, no download, no account. Ads sit next to the game and the developer's share of that revenue is 70%.

### And this one none of them has: you publish a game by talking to an assistant

klyo games is wired into AI assistants through the open MCP protocol: 45 ready commands, twenty of them for game developers. You tell your assistant “prepare this game on klyo” and it creates the entry, uploads the package, checks the state and reads back the statistics. You release it to the catalogue yourself, with one button, because software can tell that a package is complete but not that the game is playable.

[See how to connect an assistant](https://games.klyo.pl/mcp/)

## Where this came from

klyo is a small Polish company that builds websites and keeps them running on its own servers. We started writing games for ourselves, as proof that something running in a browser can be fast and comfortable on a phone too. Once the first games were finished, it turned out the hard part was not writing them.

A developer finishes a game and has nowhere to show it. On forums the post waits for a moderator or disappears as self-promotion. File services give you an address but you have to bring the players yourself. At the big portals a submission sits for months. This portal exists so that road takes an afternoon instead of half a year.

## What it means for a player

- **Click and play**: The game opens in a browser tab. No store, no download, no ad video before the start.
- **No account needed**: You sign up for nothing in order to play. An account only matters once you want your score on the leaderboard, a rating, or a review.
- **Same on a phone**: Same address, same game, touch controls. Nothing is installed and nothing stays behind after you close the tab.
- **Your progress comes back**: The save stays on your device, and once you sign in it travels between computer and phone.
- **A small window on top**: A game can be detached into a small window next to your work, and in the Chrome and Edge browsers on a computer it stays above other programs. Nothing is installed for this.
- **You record on your own device**: You record a short clip of your play with our tool in the browser, with no software at all. You can add your voice and your face, and the recording stays on your device until you decide to show it.
- **No video before the game**: If there is an ad, it sits next to the game, never before it. Only the developer can place a break inside the game, and the developer gets a share of it.
- **Leaderboards and records**: Games with the klyo SDK have boards for the day, the week and all time, and the best scores reach the records hub. Only a score from a real round counts.

## What it means for a developer

- **Your game is up the same day**: Upload a package or paste the address of a game that already exists somewhere. After a check the game gets its own page and a place in the catalogue.
- **Moving a game is one address**: If your game already sits on another service, you do not have to package it again. You paste the address, we fetch the files and ask you to confirm the game is yours.
- **The rights stay yours**: We do not buy the game and we do not ask for exclusivity. The same game can stay everywhere it is today.
- **Your share is 70%**: Ads sit next to the games. 70% of the ad revenue attributed to your games is yours, paid out from 100 PLN. You pay us nothing to publish.
- **You can see what is happening**: Day-by-day statistics, ratings signed by the person who left them, and reviews that land straight in your inbox.
- **Your own address**: The game has its own address you can send and paste anywhere, and search engines see it from day one.

## How we make money

From ads next to the games and from hosting, which is our main business. We do not sell player data, we charge nothing for publishing a game and we take no upfront cut from developers.

## What stage we are at

The catalogue is young: 20 games from 7 developers, every one looked at by a person before it reached the shelf. We are not going to dress that up: a portal that promises a developer thousands of players on day one is lying, and it shows within a week.

## Common questions

### Are the games free?

Yes, every game in the catalogue runs free and without an account. The portal is paid for by the ads next to the games.

### Do I need an account?

Not to play. An account is only needed for your score to reach the leaderboard, to rate a game or write a review, and for your save to travel between phone and computer.

### Can I publish my own game?

Yes. The catalogue is open to anyone with a game that runs in a browser. You upload a package or paste the address where the game already lives, and we check it before letting it in.

### How much does a developer earn?

70% of the ad revenue attributed to their games, paid out from 100 PLN. Settlement starts with the first month in which ads next to games bring revenue. We publish the share and promise no figures.

### Does the game have to be exclusive to you?

No. We ask for no exclusivity and take no rights. The same game can sit on other services at the same time.

### Is it a safe place for a child?

Games are reviewed by a person before publication and the catalogue carries age labels. A game runs in a separated frame, so it cannot reach the rest of the browser. Posts and reviews pass through a filter, and any content can be reported with one click.
