# How to delete your klyo games account

Delete your account yourself in the klyo games app: avatar, Settings, Privacy and data, Delete account. On games.klyo.pl the same button sits at the bottom of your profile. Deletion takes effect at once and cannot be undone. If you cannot sign in, write to kontakt@klyo.pl.

- 3 min read
- Steps: 5

### On this page

## Before you start

- The klyo games account you sign in with: Apple, Google or an email address.
- The klyo games app on iPhone or Android, or any browser with games.klyo.pl.
- A few extra minutes if you want to download your data in one file first.

## Step by step

1. **Open the settings in the app**: Tap your avatar in the top right corner of the start screen, then “Settings”. Scroll to the “Privacy and data” section. It has two buttons: “Download my data” and “Delete account”.
2. **Download your data if you want to keep it**: Tap “Download my data”. You get one file with everything we keep with your account: profile, scores, rank, medals, posts and settings. Save it on your phone or send it to yourself.
3. **Delete the account and confirm**: Tap “Delete account”, type the words “delete my account” in the box and confirm. The account disappears at once from all your devices, and the app goes back to playing as a guest.
4. **The same on the website**: Sign in on games.klyo.pl, open your profile and scroll to the very bottom, to the part about your data. Click “Delete account”, type the confirmation words shown in the box and confirm. The result is the same as in the app.
5. **Without access to the account**: If you cannot sign in, write to kontakt@klyo.pl from the account's email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, give your player name and how you sign in. We delete the account and confirm it in our reply.

## If something goes wrong

### What goes at once

The account and its sign in email, your player name, the name from your Apple or Google account, rank, points, day streak and medals, hearts and followed creators, game saves kept with the account, league and tournament data and your viewing trail. The account also leaves the app on every phone.

### What stays and for how long

Community posts and comments stay without an author, so other people's conversations are not broken. Scores on the leaderboards stay under the nickname, with no link to the account. For 30 days we keep only a trace that the account existed, so a mistake can be undone. Creator settlement documents stay for 5 years from the end of the tax year, as the law requires.

### I want my scores and posts gone too

Delete a post, comment or clip yourself before deleting the account, next to each of them. The “Delete this device's data” button in the rank window on the website clears your leaderboard scores. You can also write to kontakt@klyo.pl and we remove them for you.

### Data on your phone

The app keeps its random number, downloaded games and draft recordings on your phone. They go away with the app, and you can also remove downloaded games one by one in your profile, under Downloaded.

## Can I undo an account deletion?

Not by yourself. For 30 days we still keep a trace that the account existed, so if it was a mistake, write to kontakt@klyo.pl right away.

## Does deleting the app delete my account?

No. Deleting the app only clears the data on your phone. The account stays until you delete it in the settings or on the website.

## Do I need an account to play?

No. Every game works without an account, and your scores then stay with the device. An account adds a name on the leaderboards, the community and progress on every device.
