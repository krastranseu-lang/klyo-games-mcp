# Everything *klyo games* can do

One place to play browser games, swipe through clips, talk about games and publish your own. 86 features work today and 3 are in development. Each one is checked against our code every time the site is built.

[Play now](https://games.klyo.pl/)

[Publish your game](https://games.klyo.pl/developer-studio/)

- **86** features that work today, each backed by our code
- **20** tools for AI assistants over the open MCP protocol
- **0 PLN** to play, and to publish your own game
- **EN and PL** every page of the portal in English and Polish

## Play

Games start from a link, on a phone or a computer, with nothing to install.

[Open the game catalogue](https://games.klyo.pl/)

- **One tap to play**: A game opens in the tab you already have. No store, no download, no account and no video ad before it starts.
- **Made for phones**: Full screen opens with the same tap that starts the game, touch controls work at once, and a landscape game on an upright phone gets a rotated frame.
- **Mini game window**: Pop a game out into a small window next to your work. In Chrome and Edge on a computer it stays on top of everything else.
- **Progress on every device**: Games with the klyo SDK save progress on our server, and once you sign in it moves with you from phone to computer.
- **Search and shelves**: Search by name, genre or mood, browse demos and games in development, and open a shelf picked for kids.
- **Ratings and reviews**: Rate a game and change your mind later. Reviews appear under the game, and the developer can reply right below.
- **Notifications**: The bell collects hearts, ratings, comments and new followers. Allow push notifications and they reach you outside the portal too.
- **Works like an app**: Add klyo games to your home screen and it opens full screen, like an app from a store.
- **English and Polish, light and dark**: The whole portal speaks English and Polish, and one switch picks a light or dark look.
- **One account for everything**: The same account plays and publishes: scores, rank, clips, followers and settings in one place. [Your profile](https://games.klyo.pl/profile/)

## Clips

Short gameplay videos you swipe through like TikTok, recorded right in the browser.

[Watch the clips](https://games.klyo.pl/clips/)

- **Vertical clip feed**: Swipe through short clips of real gameplay. When a game grabs you, one tap and you are playing it.
- **A feed that learns on your device**: A clip watched to the end brings similar ones closer and a skipped one pushes them away. That learning stays in your browser.
- **Comments beside the clip**: Open the conversation without stopping the video: a sheet slides up on a phone, a side column opens on a computer.
- **Record in the browser**: Press Record a clip next to the game and install nothing. On a computer it works with any game, on a phone with games that use the klyo SDK. [Guide: record a clip](https://games.klyo.pl/support/record-gameplay-clip/)
- **Keep the last 30 seconds**: With recording on, play as long as you like. When something great happens, one button keeps the last 30 seconds.
- **Cut the best moment**: A strip of frames with two handles. A game can mark its own big moments, so the best part is selected for you.
- **Captions from your voice**: Talk while you play and captions appear in time with your words. Your voice becomes text on our server and is deleted right after. Every caption can be edited.
- **Caption styles and themes**: The Classic, Neon, Game and TikTok themes set captions, the face frame and animations in one tap. Font, colour, outline and entrance are yours to change.
- **Your reaction in the frame**: Turn on the camera and react in a frame over the gameplay, live or after recording by playing the clip back and filming just your face.
- **Add your voice later**: Recorded without a microphone? Play the clip from the start of your selection and talk over it, in sync with the picture.
- **Automatic bleep**: A swear word in your voice is covered with a short tone in the finished clip and hidden in the captions.
- **Hands-free start and stop**: Pick your own words for START and STOP and control recording by voice, hands on the game. Your device recognises the words itself.
- **Drafts stay with you**: Recordings wait on your device until you post them yourself. You can also save a clip as a file, no account needed. [Your recordings](https://games.klyo.pl/recordings/)
- **Captions in two languages**: A clip carries its captions as text, so viewers read them in English or Polish. Your browser can draft the second version for you to check.

## Community

Posts, clips and conversations around the games, with moderation you can see.

[Visit the community](https://games.klyo.pl/community/)

- **Posts with a game attached**: Share a playtest, a launch, a clip or a question. Attach a game from the catalogue and its card appears next to your post.
- **Comments, votes and hearts**: Reply in threads, vote up what helps, and edit or delete your own comments whenever you like.
- **A conversation page for every game**: Every game has its own discussion page that search engines can read, linked from the game page.
- **Follow developers**: Follow a developer and hear about a new version of their game before you stumble on it.
- **Report and block**: Report a post, comment or clip in two taps. A blocked account disappears from your lists, your clips and your notifications.
- **Moderation you can see**: Three credible reports hide a post until a person decides. Each decision has a reason, and the numbers go into a public report. [Moderation report](https://games.klyo.pl/moderation-report/)

## Records and ranks

Every score comes from a real round, and every player climbs the same ladder.

[See today's records](https://games.klyo.pl/records/)

- **Records hub**: All-time records and podiums for every game, a medal table of the day and a live strip of the newest records.
- **Day, week and all-time boards**: A game with the klyo SDK gets boards for today, this week and all time, and players see their place and the person just above them. [Guide: add a leaderboard](https://games.klyo.pl/support/add-leaderboard/)
- **Scores you cannot type in**: A score counts only from a round the server started and signed, after at least 3 seconds of play and under the limit the developer set.
- **17 ranks, from Recruit to General**: You earn XP for playing, scores, medals and conversation, never for paying. XP never goes down.
- **Know who just passed you**: While you play, the page tells you live that someone has just beaten your score for today, and who it was.
- **Player pages and ranking**: Every player with an account has a public page with rank and records, and a place in the players ranking. [Players ranking](https://games.klyo.pl/player/)

## Play together

Rooms, quick matches and a ready-made lobby, run by klyo so the developer writes only the game.

[How to add multiplayer](https://games.klyo.pl/game-sdk/)

- **Rooms with a code**: Create a room and send friends a 6-character code or a link. A turn-based room holds up to 64 players, a live one up to 16.
- **Quick match**: One tap and you are playing someone with a similar record of wins.
- **A lobby drawn by the portal**: Waiting rooms, top players and your record live in a portal window, so a game gets a lobby with one call.
- **Direct connection**: Real-time games connect players to each other directly (WebRTC), with klyo only introducing them.
- **Wins ranking and match resume**: Finished matches build a wins ranking for each game, and after a page refresh you return to your match with every move.

## Developer Studio

Everything from uploading a game to its first players, at dev.klyo.pl.

[Tour the Developer Studio](https://games.klyo.pl/developer-studio/)

- **Upload a ZIP**: A ZIP with index.html inside or a single HTML file, up to 100 MB. Large packages upload in chunks, so a dropped connection keeps your progress. [Guide: publish a game](https://games.klyo.pl/support/publish-html5-game/)
- **Move a game from the web**: Paste the address of a game on itch.io, GameJolt, Newgrounds, GitHub Pages, Netlify or your own server. We confirm it is yours and bring the files over. [Move your game](https://games.klyo.pl/move-your-game/)
- **Automatic check**: Every package is checked for size, missing files, paths, third-party ad networks and traces of malicious code.
- **Your AI fills in the form**: One button and Claude, ChatGPT or Claude Code types the name, tagline and description while you watch. You tick the content survey yourself. [AI assistants and MCP](https://games.klyo.pl/mcp/)
- **Play it first**: Try the game in desktop and phone shapes, in full screen, or on your own phone by scanning a code.
- **Measured on four screens**: At release we open the game on a phone upright and sideways, on a tablet and on a computer, and show you what a player will see.
- **Clip and cover from your play**: Play for a moment in the studio while we record in the background. That round becomes the tile clip, and you pick the cover from its frames.
- **Description in English and Polish**: English is required, Polish earns extra XP, and your own AI assistant proposes other languages. You approve every translation yourself.
- **An age label from a survey**: Answer ten yes or no questions about the content and get an age label on both the PEGI and the Apple scale.
- **Demo and in-development labels**: Show an unfinished game with an honest label and a deadline of 7 or 30 days for the next version.
- **New versions and one-button rollback**: Drop a new ZIP on the game card, play it and release it in one click. The previous version stays with us, one button away. [Guide: release a new version](https://games.klyo.pl/support/release-new-version/)
- **An answer for a white screen**: When a game does not start, the studio lists the files it asked for and the errors it threw in the browser, in order. [Guide: white screen](https://games.klyo.pl/support/white-screen/)
- **Through Google's eyes**: See your game page the way a search engine does: a verdict first, then the tasks that would lift it.
- **30 days of analytics**: Visits and plays day by day, counted on our side without identifying players.
- **Reply to reviews**: Reviews of your games reach the studio, and you can answer each one publicly under the game.
- **Your creator page**: Your name next to every game, your own address, a tagline for search and links to your other profiles, each one verified.
- **App Store and Google Play badges**: If your game is also a phone app, paste the store links and the badges appear on the game page.
- **A badge for your own site**: A short code for your own site that leads straight to your game on klyo.
- **Event record**: Download every publication, edit and moderation decision with its date, as a spreadsheet file.
- **Talk to the team**: A private conversation with the klyo team inside the studio, with a screenshot or a code patch attached.
- **Payouts: 70% of ad revenue**: Developers will get 70% of the net ad revenue next to their games, paid out from 100 PLN. It starts on the day Google approves ads on the portal. In development

## AI assistants

Publish, fix and check your game by talking to the assistant you already use.

[Connect your AI assistant](https://games.klyo.pl/mcp/)

- **MCP server with twenty tools for developers**: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor and VS Code publish and manage your games through the open Model Context Protocol.
- **You have the last word**: The assistant prepares the game, but you accept the terms, you tick the survey and you release the game to the catalogue. It never sees your money.
- **Fixes without a ZIP**: Your assistant reads the game's files and patches them in a draft version. Players do not see it until you play it and release it.
- **Your agent sees inside the game**: Diagnostics hand your assistant the missing files and browser errors, so a white screen turns into one clear sentence.
- **Starter files for a new game**: Ask for a new game and get working files built on the Klyo Kit, with menu, pause and sound from the first minute.
- **API key for scripts and CI**: Generate a key in the studio and publish from your own script or build pipeline. We store only a hash of it. [Publishing without uploading](https://games.klyo.pl/publish-without-uploading/)
- **Plugin, extension and skill**: Install klyo as a Claude Code plugin, a Gemini CLI extension or an agent skill from our public GitHub repository.
- **In the official MCP registry**: The server is listed in the Model Context Protocol registry as pl.klyo/games, with the domain proven by a signature in DNS.
- **Readable by machines**: llms.txt, a Markdown copy of every page, an MCP server card, an agent catalogue and a game index with age labels.
- **Tools for agents in the browser**: The portal describes its search and pages as WebMCP tools, so an agent in the browser uses them without guessing.

## klyo SDK and Kit

One script connects a game to scores, saves, ads, clips and multiplayer.

[Read the SDK reference](https://games.klyo.pl/game-sdk/)

- **One line in your game**: Add one script and the game reports scores, saves progress, asks for an ad break or a rewarded ad, requests full screen and lets players record clips.
- **Cloud saves up to 64 KB**: Per player and per game, stored on our server and shared across all of the player's devices.
- **Klyo Kit**: A game skeleton with menu, pause, end screen, settings, touch, mouse, keyboard and gamepad, sound without files and effects. You write only the gameplay.
- **Test on your machine first**: klyo-test measures your game exactly as we do at release, including on a slow phone and in both languages.
- **Works outside klyo too**: Open the same build anywhere else and nothing breaks: an ad break returns at once, and scores and saves stay in the browser.

## Safety and trust

People review the games, age labels are clear, and every decision can be appealed.

[Read the moderation report](https://games.klyo.pl/moderation-report/)

- **A person reviews every game**: After release someone at klyo plays every new developer game. Until then it opens only for adult accounts, and ads next to it stay off.
- **Every game in its own sandbox**: Each game runs at its own address in a separated frame, so it cannot reach your account or other games.
- **No outside scripts in games**: A game that sends data to someone else's server or carries its own ad network does not pass the check.
- **Clear age labels**: Every game carries an age label, published on both the PEGI and the Apple scale, and games for adults never show ads.
- **People first on kids' games**: Under games rated 12 and younger, a person approves each comment before anyone else can see it.
- **Report it and a person decides in 24 hours**: Games, posts, comments and clips have a report button, and a person decides within 24 hours of a credible report. [Report content](https://games.klyo.pl/report/)
- **A button for parents**: Every game page has a report for a parent or guardian, with no account and no details about the child.
- **Public moderation report**: How many reports came in, what we decided and how fast, published for anyone to read.
- **Your data, your call**: Download everything we hold about you as one file, or delete your account yourself, at once.
- **A real company behind it**: klyo is a Polish company with its tax number, address and name in the footer of every page.

## Common questions

### What is klyo games?

A free portal of browser games with a vertical clip feed, a community, records and ranks, and a studio where independent developers publish HTML5 games the same day. It is run by klyo, a Polish software company.

### Do I need an account to play?

No. Games start from a link without an account. An account adds your name on leaderboards, ratings, comments, clips and progress that follows you between devices.

### Can an AI assistant publish a game on klyo games?

Yes. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor and VS Code connect to the klyo MCP server, which has twenty tools for game developers. The assistant uploads and prepares the game, and you accept the terms and release it to the catalogue yourself.

### How do clips work?

A player or developer records gameplay in the browser, cuts the best moment, adds captions from their voice or their face, and posts it to the vertical feed. Until it is posted, the recording stays on their device.

### What is not ready yet?

In development: payouts for developers, apps for iPhone and Android and portal pages in more languages. Each of these is marked as in development here until it works for everyone.

### How do you know these features really work?

Every entry on this list points to a piece of our code. Each time the site is built we check that the code is still there. If it is gone, the site does not build until this page is corrected.
